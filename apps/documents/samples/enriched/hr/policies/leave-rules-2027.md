# Leave rules 2027

_Source: Confluence · HR / Policies · last modified 20 November 2026_

These rules replace the leave rules of 2026. Questions go to the HR team.

## Rule 1: Requesting leave

**Rule:** Leave must be requested in the self-service portal before it starts.
**Kind:** obligation
**Applies to:** all permanent staff, temporary staff
**Condition:** The leave lasts more than half a day.
**Effective from:** 1 January 2027

### Exceptions

- Sick leave, which is reported by phone on the first day

## Rule 2: Working from abroad

**Rule:** Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days a year.
**Kind:** permission
**Applies to:** all permanent staff
**Condition:** The manager agrees and the country is on the approved list.
**Effective from:** 1 March 2027

### Exceptions

- Roles that handle customer cash
- Countries under a travel warning

## Rule 3: Carry-over of leave

**Rule:** Unused leave may be carried over to the next year, up to five days.
**Kind:** permission
**Applies to:** all permanent staff
**Condition:** The days are taken before 31 March of the next year.
**Effective from:** 1 January 2027

### Exceptions

- Leave that could not be taken because of long illness

## Rule 4: Private use of company laptops

**Rule:** Company laptops must not be used for private business or shared with family members.
**Kind:** prohibition
**Applies to:** all staff, contractors
**Condition:** The laptop is issued by the company.
**Effective from:** 1 February 2027

### Exceptions

- Short private browsing during breaks

## Rule 5: Approval of leave

**Rule:** Managers must answer a leave request within 5 working days.
**Kind:** obligation
**Applies to:** managers
**Condition:** The request was sent through the portal.
**Effective from:** 1 January 2027

### Exceptions

- Requests sent in the last week of December
